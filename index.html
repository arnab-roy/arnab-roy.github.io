Systems

Selected work at Mysten Labs involving cryptography, distributed protocols, programming languages, and formal verification.

  • Authentication

    zkLogin ↗

    Privacy-preserving blockchain authentication using existing web credentials. Talk at Stanford ↗

    • System Wallet setup complicates onboarding and creates tradeoffs between usability and security. zkLogin combines OAuth credentials, ephemeral keys, and zero-knowledge proofs to provide familiar account access without placing personal data onchain or giving up non-custodial ownership. It is deployed as a native Sui primitive and has been presented at CCS, SBC, and Real World Cryptography.
    • Contributions Co-inventor, research prototype and product collaboration, MPC ceremony lead, and publication co-lead.
  • Post-quantum

    Securing Sui in the Quantum Computing Era ↗

    A system-wide post-quantum transition strategy for Sui’s cryptographic stack.

    • System Quantum attacks affect signatures, encryption, hashes, proof systems, and distributed protocols differently, requiring a coordinated migration for a live network. The crypto-agile framework covers NIST-standard signatures and encryption, hash parameters, proof systems, DKG, and backward-compatible account migration. The public roadmap distinguishes immediate risks from longer-term migrations across the Sui stack.
    • Contributions Research lead for the transition analysis and co-developer of the PQ-EdDSA ownership proof for backward-compatible account migration.
  • Data infrastructure

    Seal ↗

    Programmable access control for encrypted application data.

    • System Permissions duplicated across identity, gateways, services, and databases increase system complexity and the consequences of configuration errors. Seal uses threshold encryption to keep data encrypted until key servers verify programmable Move policies for applications such as AI data, subscriptions, licensing, and messaging. It connects application policy with cryptographic enforcement as a developer-facing Sui primitive.
    • Contributions Design coauthor and lead for the cryptographic formalization, whitepaper, and security audit.
  • Bitcoin finance

    Hashi ↗

    A decentralized collateralization primitive for native Bitcoin.

    • System Wrapped assets and opaque custodial lending make custody, leverage, and credit health difficult to verify. Hashi is an MPC-secured, smart-contract-controlled primitive for Bitcoin-backed lending and credit, with observable loan terms and automated lifecycle management. It progressed from research architecture to public testnet with integrations across custodians, wallets, and lending protocols.
    • Contributions Lead for cryptographic formalization and the whitepaper, with design and prototyping of research components.
  • Private assets

    Confidential Transfers ↗

    Encrypted balances and transfers with controlled auditability.

    • System Transparent ledgers expose balances and transaction values, while fully opaque systems can fail compliance and audit requirements. Confidential Transfers uses encrypted balances and transfer amounts for payments, payroll, treasury, and regulated workflows, supported by optimized protocols and machine-checked reasoning. It has been released in public beta, with further protocol optimizations accompanied by Lean proofs.
    • Contributions Co-designer, whitepaper and formalization lead, with prototyping and Lean-verified optimizations.
