Products

From cryptographic research to production systems

At Mysten Labs, I have worked on systems at the intersection of cryptography, distributed protocols, programming languages, and formal verification.

01 / Authentication

zkLogin ↗

Privacy-preserving blockchain authentication using existing web credentials.

Problem
Wallet installation, seed phrases, and custodial workarounds cause users to abandon onboarding and force builders to trade usability for security.
Approach
A protocol-native authentication primitive combining OAuth credentials, ephemeral keys, and zero-knowledge proofs without placing personal data onchain.
Application scope
Consumer applications that need familiar account access while retaining non-custodial ownership and protocol-level security.
Result
Deployed as a native Sui primitive and presented through peer-reviewed and research venues including CCS, SBC, and RWC.

Role Co-inventor ● research prototype and product collaborator ● MPC ceremony lead ● publication co-lead.

02 / Data infrastructure

Seal ↗

Programmable access control for encrypted application data.

Problem
Product permissions are duplicated across identity, gateways, services, and databases, expanding both complexity and the blast radius of mistakes.
Approach
Threshold encryption with programmable Move policies: data remains encrypted until key servers verify the application’s rules.
Application scope
AI data, subscriptions, licensing, messaging, time-locked content, and other applications requiring conditional disclosure.
Result
A developer-facing primitive that connects application policy with cryptographic enforcement across the Sui stack.

Role Design coauthor ● cryptographic formalization, whitepaper and security-audit lead.

03 / Bitcoin finance

Hashi ↗

A decentralized collateralization primitive for native Bitcoin.

Problem
Wrapped assets and opaque custodial lending leave holders unable to verify custody, leverage, or credit health.
Approach
An MPC-secured, smart-contract-controlled primitive for native BTC, with observable loan terms and automated lifecycle management.
Application scope
Bitcoin-backed lending, structured products, and institutional credit without requiring synthetic BTC or a centralized balance sheet.
Result
The design progressed from research architecture to public testnet, with integrations spanning custodians, wallets, and lending protocols.

Role Cryptographic-formalization and whitepaper lead ● design and prototyping of novel research components.

04 / Private assets

Confidential Transfers ↗

Encrypted balances and transfers with controlled auditability.

Problem
Transparent ledgers expose balances and transaction values, while fully opaque systems can fail compliance and audit requirements.
Approach
Encrypted balances and transfer amounts with controlled auditability, supported by optimized protocols and machine-checked reasoning.
Application scope
Payments, payroll, treasury, institutional assets, and regulated workflows that require transaction confidentiality.
Result
Released in public beta, with further protocol optimizations accompanied by Lean proofs.

Role Co-designer ● whitepaper/formalization lead ● prototyping and Lean-verified optimizations.

05 / Network security

Securing Sui in the Quantum Computing Era ↗

A system-wide post-quantum transition strategy for Sui’s cryptographic stack.

Problem
Quantum attacks affect signatures, encryption, hashes, proof systems, and distributed protocols in different ways. A live network needs a coordinated migration across all of these components.
Approach
A crypto-agile transition framework covering NIST-standard signatures and encryption, hash parameters, post-quantum proof systems, DKG, and backward-compatible account migration.
Application scope
Accounts, transaction integrity, zkLogin, random beacons, secure channels, confidential data, and application-level cryptographic primitives across Sui.
Result
A public technical roadmap that distinguishes immediate risks from longer-term migrations and identifies practical transition strategies for each part of the stack.

Role Research lead for the system-wide transition analysis ● co-developed the PQ-EdDSA ownership proof for backward-compatible account migration.

03

Research

Cryptography, formal reasoning, and optimization

My research focuses on problems where formal guarantees, algorithmic choices, and implementation constraints must be considered together, from cryptographic protocols and secure systems to optimization and specialized computing.

Recent work

  • Batch threshold encryption. Developing efficient constructions based on partial-fraction techniques, with applications to decentralized protocols.
  • Post-quantum migration. Studying seed-based ownership proofs and migration strategies for accounts with exposed or dormant keys.
  • Verified confidential systems. Developing protocol optimizations and machine-checked Lean proofs for their critical properties.

Cryptography and secure systems

Research on zero-knowledge proofs, threshold encryption, signatures, authentication, privacy, and post-quantum cryptography. The work ranges from new constructions and security arguments to formalization, auditing, and deployment.

Formal methods and protocol security

Research on computational protocol security, Protocol Composition Logic, inductive proofs, automated decision procedures, and machine-checked reasoning, beginning with my Stanford Ph.D. work on network protocols.

Learning and optimization on specialized hardware

Research on reformulating clustering, outlier detection, matrix factorization, higher-order optimization, and local search for Ising and quantum-inspired processors, often using hybrid methods that combine classical algorithms with specialized hardware.
